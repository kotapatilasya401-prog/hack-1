QUANTUMGUARD
Quantum-Enhanced Digital Payment Fraud Detection

PRACTICE RESULTS

This file contains preliminary practice observations.

Dataset:
- Synthetic transaction dataset
- 15 sample transactions
- No real customer information used

Classical component:
- Rule-based baseline implemented for initial testing.
- Risk score is calculated from demonstration transaction features.

Quantum component:
- Qiskit quantum circuit implemented.
- Two qubits are used in the practice circuit.
- Transaction amount is encoded into a rotation angle.
- Circuit measurement results are collected locally.
